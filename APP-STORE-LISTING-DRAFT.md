# CreditFlow Shopify App Store listing draft

Status: draft for product and policy review; not published.

## Positioning

**Shopify Native Store Credit rewards with refund-aware automation.**

CreditFlow helps merchants set order-based cashback rules, issue Shopify Native Store Credit, reverse rewards when orders are refunded, and send branded transactional emails. Merchants can preview rules and review processing activity before they depend on an automation.

## Short description

Automate cashback rewards with Shopify Native Store Credit, refund-aware reversals and branded customer emails.

## Feature bullets

- Create fixed or percentage reward rules with product eligibility, order and customer conditions, minimums, caps and expiry.
- Preview example orders and save new reward rules paused until you are ready to activate them.
- Keep customer credit balances in Shopify Native Store Credit.
- Reverse the relevant portion of rewards when an order is refunded, with records for reconciliation.
- Customize credit-issued emails and optional 7, 3 or 1 day expiry reminders on eligible plans.
- See reward activity, queue status and items that need attention.
- Choose and manage plans through Shopify App Pricing.
